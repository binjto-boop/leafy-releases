Leafy is a vocabulary app for macOS. Press <kbd>⌥A</kbd> over any word on screen, read the definition where you found it, and it is already saved.

It reads what is on the screen rather than what is in a text field, so it works in PDFs, ebooks, scanned pages, images, subtitles and anything else you can see.

## How it works

|  | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| <img src="https://leafyapp.uk/images/readme/lookup.gif" width="300"> | **Look it up.** Press <kbd>⌥A</kbd> and box any word. Leafy reads the whole sentence around it, so the meaning you get is the one that fits where you found it, with pronunciation and a second example underneath. |
| <img src="https://leafyapp.uk/images/readme/library.gif" width="300"> | **It saves itself.** Every lookup lands in a searchable library with the sentence attached, sorted into folders you choose. Nothing to type up afterwards. |
| <img src="https://leafyapp.uk/images/readme/review.gif" width="300"> | **Review writes itself.** Leafy blanks the word out of the sentence you first met it in and asks you to fill the gap. A few each day, no decks to build. |

## What you get

- **Scan anything on screen.** PDFs, ebooks, images, subtitles, scanned text.
- **Definitions in context.** The sentence you found the word in shapes the meaning shown.
- **A library that builds itself.** Every lookup is saved, searchable, and yours to organise.
- **Review that writes itself.** Fill in the blank, built from your own reading.
- **Import and export.** JSON, CSV, Excel, PDF and Anki, plus templates for bringing a list in from another app.
- **PopClip support.** Define, Translate and Save straight from the PopClip bar.
- **Menu bar mode.** Runs without a Dock icon if you want it out of the way.

## Languages

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Greek, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Thai and Arabic, with more on the way.

The interface itself is available in 16 languages.

## Privacy

No account and no ads. Your words are stored on your Mac, in a plain JSON file you can read and back up yourself. Usage statistics are anonymous and can be switched off. Full details are in the [privacy policy](https://leafyapp.uk/privacy).

## Install

Download the latest `.dmg` from [Releases](https://github.com/binjto-boop/leafy-releases/releases/latest), drag Leafy to Applications, and grant Screen Recording when asked. That permission is what lets Leafy read the words on your screen, and nothing leaves your Mac because of it.

Updates arrive in the app. Quit with <kbd>⌘Q</kbd> and reopen to pick one up.

Having trouble? The [help pages](https://leafyapp.uk/help) cover the common ones, including [why a scan can come back empty](https://leafyapp.uk/help/no-text-found) and [Screen Recording going stale after an update](https://leafyapp.uk/help/screen-recording-permission).

## About this repository

This repository hosts the released builds and their notes. Leafy itself is closed source. Every version's changes are listed under [Releases](https://github.com/binjto-boop/leafy-releases/releases), and there is a readable history on the [updates page](https://leafyapp.uk/update).

Only the current version is available for download. Older builds are refused by the update service anyway, so running one is not possible.

Found a bug or want to ask for something? [Open an issue](https://github.com/binjto-boop/leafy-releases/issues) or come to [Discord](https://discord.gg/cr3mHVtT5j).
